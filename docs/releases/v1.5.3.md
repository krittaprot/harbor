# Harbor v1.5.3

A quieter launch and clearer, friendlier software updates.

## Menu-bar-only launch

- Harbor now starts in the menu bar with no Dock or Command-Tab entry.
- The dashboard no longer opens automatically on launch or after an update restart.
- Click Harbor’s menu-bar icon and choose **Open Harbor** or **Settings** whenever needed.
  The dashboard opens without adding a Dock icon; closing it keeps monitoring active.

## Friendlier update feedback

- An animated orbit shows that Harbor is checking GitHub, with a clear checking message.
- A green animated checkmark and **“You’re all set!”** confirm a successful up-to-date check.
- **“A new update is ready”** clearly identifies the available version and installation action.
- Successful checks show their time. Failed or pending checks never claim the app is current.
- Download, signature verification, installation and restart have distinct messages.
  Light/dark appearance, keyboard focus and Reduce Motion are supported.

## Update from Harbor

On v1.5.2 or newer, open **Settings → Software updates → Check for Updates**, choose
**Download & Install**, then **Restart Harbor**. After restarting, look for Harbor in the
menu bar; it will not open a dashboard or appear in the Dock automatically.

Your settings and monitored services stay in place. For manual installation or v1.5.1 and
earlier, download `Harbor_1.5.3_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `Harbor_1.5.3_x64.dmg` (Intel),
quit Harbor, replace it in its existing Applications location, and reopen it.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. Updater downloads are signature-verified;
`SHA256SUMS` covers the release assets. Existing scan-visibility and machine-specific-default
limitations remain unchanged.
